# Documentation check against the four CVM runs, 2026-11-20

A check of every tracked document after the four CVM flow runs: TDX Option 1 and 2
([`cvm-flow-rehearsal-2026-11-18.md`](cvm-flow-rehearsal-2026-11-18.md)), and SEV-SNP verity and initrd
([`cvm-flow-rehearsal-2026-11-19.md`](cvm-flow-rehearsal-2026-11-19.md)). The question was whether following the
documents gets someone through. Four questions:

- **A.** Is what stopped or slowed those runs written where a reader meets it?
- **B.** Do quoted values, paths, commands, flags, exit codes and commits match the repository?
- **C.** Can someone with no prior knowledge get from start to end, and are the stuck items of the 2026-11-15 trial
  solved?
- **D.** Do the documents point at each other correctly, and use the same names?

The records in `docs/results/` were checked only for their references: what they say happened is not corrected.

The repository was at `996ce7a` when the check started. Checked by reading the documents against the code, with the
values of the four runs, `go list -deps` and `git log`. No guest or instance was started. `docs/status/night-log.md`
and `docs/status/cvm-port.md` are not tracked and were not checked.

### B. Statements the repository contradicts

- **The guides:**
  - "the same modes; `build` normalises them": only Option 2 sets every mode (755). Option 1 keeps the execute bit
    (0644 or 0755).
  - `umask 022` was the old script's. `verity.go` sets the modes explicitly.
  - `<auditor-guest-ip>` was said to come from the Vendor. The Auditor reads it from its own `status`.
  - "A1 to A9 instead of step 11, then step 12" ran step 12 twice: A9 is step 12.
  - The SEV-SNP recorder was called as `./record`. It is built as `record-snp`.
  - The guest tables named the image family as "the image". The script resolves the family once to an exact version.
- **`settings-and-hashes.md`** said "one setting" changes a Nitro image. `build` also repacks it, empties `/tmp`,
  removes logs and caches, and adds the responder's files.
- **Nitro, since `89c0063`:**
  - `aws-nitro.md`, `reproduce.md` and `ra-comparison.md` said only a change to code the responder can run moves
    PCR0 and PCR2. What `build` adds to the image moves them too.
  - `aws-nitro.md` called the `6146c10` values "the reference".
  - It named the role folders as where the defaults live (shared since `53bfd5e`).
  - It listed nowhere the files `build` layers in (`/att-app-cmd` was in no document).
  - It did not say that the default memory passes 8192 MiB for an EIF over 2 GiB.
  - The qixiangme docs commits (009440b, 1ae0d03) are ported in `b3d078c`: `WORKDIR` among the image settings, and
    what `launch` does above 8 GiB.
- **`STRUCTURE.md`** says it lists every folder. It left out `.github/`, the two subfolders of `docs/results/`, and
  `SECURITY.md` and the licences. Its note on what `att-user` links left out `chain/register/auditor`
  (`go list -deps ./cmd/user`).
- **`cmd/auditor/README.md`:** `att-auditor ra-serve` answers "on the guest on TDX and SEV-SNP". It takes only `nsm`
  or `fixture=`; the guests run `att-ra-responder`.
- **`cmd/README.md`** did not say that exit 12 is `att-auditor`'s only.
- **`contracts/README.md`** listed PCR12 for every SEV-SNP registration. `amd-sev-snp-initrd` has five values.
- **`internal/tee/parse/snp/testdata/README.md`:**
  - It called T10 "not run yet". It ran on 2026-11-16.
  - It counted a recording as four files in one place and three in another.
- **Out of date since the CVM runs:**
  - The README's status and links.
  - The TDX and SEV-SNP T4 and T5 cells of `test-matrix.md`, and its list of the scripts that write result lines.
  - Row 2 of `build-info-gaps.md`: a public-chain run is missing for the CVM flow too.
  - `test-matrix.md`'s Nitro baseline row (stale since `89c0063`).
