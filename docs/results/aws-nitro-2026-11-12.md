**Note on `responder-check.sh`.** Twice the full script failed at its last step because this machine's network
aborted the sixth SSH connection opened within seconds ("Software caused connection abort"; the instance's sshd logged
"Connection reset by <client> [preauth]"). The script cannot tell that from a refusal, so those two lines say
`refused`. The step was then run on its own (same remote command, same check) and passed; its line's `script` field
reads `e2e/responder-check.sh (step 7 alone)` instead of the local path of the one-off wrapper.

kernel and init (here 1.5.0); it is also the PCR1 of the older fixture `internal/tee/parse/aws/testdata/fixture-my-web.cbor`.
